Rules

  • Each game, each day, the best result wins: fewest guesses (Wordle), fewest mistakes (Connections), most right (Daily Tens), highest score (Krillion).

  • Miss a game and you lose it for that day. A failed game can't win.
  • The day's winner has the most points that day.
  • Points reset on the 1st. Past months stay here.
